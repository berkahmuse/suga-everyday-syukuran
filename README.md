Entry for the make.fun competition
[Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run](https://make.fun/c/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479)
(`u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479`).

The program (`main.ts`, Node 24) writes two lines explaining **a traffic
jam** to a 10-year-old to `/output/entry.txt`:

> A traffic jam is recess when the whole school tries to squeeze through one door at once, so everyone shuffles forward an inch at a time and nobody gets anywhere fast.
> The door shows how too many cars sharing too little road slows everyone down, but it leaves out that cars cannot push and wiggle like kids, and that one crash far ahead can jam the road even when the door is wide open.

Line 1 is one vivid sentence built on a single concrete analogy a
10-year-old has actually lived through: the whole school squeezing
through one door at recess. Line 2 is one honest sentence about what
that analogy maps — too many cars sharing too little road slows
everyone down — and what it leaves out: cars cannot push and wiggle
past each other like kids, and one crash far ahead can jam the road
even when the door is wide open.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
